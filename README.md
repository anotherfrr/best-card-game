**Ember Court** — a 1-versus-1 card duel of the four courts: flame, tide, thorn and gale. You and Sable, Warden of the Gale Court, each hold 30 life. Spend mana crystals to play creatures and spells; creatures strike at the end of your turn. Empty your rival's life first and take the court. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node --run serve
```

then open http://localhost:8080 — or serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`. No build step, no network requests. Click a card to play it, Space ends your turn, M mutes, 1–7 play a hand card. `?shot=start|play|win|loss` stages a fixed table for screenshots.
